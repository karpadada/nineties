Spotify playlist sync

Spotify is the source of truth. Each sync copies the playlist's current order to the music directory, removes tracks deleted from the Spotify playlist, and reports anything Nineties cannot confidently find on YouTube Music.

Spotify supplies playlist metadata only. Audio is fetched separately from YouTube Music; only sync media you are entitled to download.

Prototype notice: Spotify's API rules restrict using its content to facilitate downloads or as input to AI. Do not publish this workflow without Spotify's approval.
